Practical checklist

Confirm data quality tier (2 or 3) before trusting any results
Use the console's Evaluate tab / Preview to sanity-check recommendations look reasonable for sample users/products
Track coverage and diversity to catch popularity-bias problems early
Instrument CTR, conversion, and revenue-per-impression in your own analytics (BigQuery + Looker Studio works well)
Run a proper A/B test with a control group before declaring success — model-side metrics can look fine while business impact is flat
